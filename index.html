Machine learning for the diabeties detection

Diabetes is a chronic condition with a growing global prevalence, demanding early detection to improve management and outcomes. Recent advancements in machine learning (ML) have created opportunities for more accurate, accessible, and scalable early detection methods for diabetes. This paper explores the application of various ML algorithms, including supervised and unsupervised learning approaches, in identifying early indicators of diabetes using patient data, such as blood glucose levels, BMI, age, lifestyle factors, and genetic predispositions.

Enhancing Short-Term Weather Nowcasting with LSTM Networks

Accurate short-term weather nowcasting is a crucial element in disaster prevention, agriculture, transportation, and public safety. Traditional numerical weather prediction (NWP) models, although comprehensive, require significant computational power and often struggle with very short lead-time forecasts due to model complexity and update latency. In recent years, data-driven models, particularly deep learning techniques, have gained prominence for their ability to model complex, non-linear relationships in real-time weather data.
